Audio Splicer

Merge, cut and paste audio. Nothing leaves your device.

Editing: none · Clipboard: empty

How it works: click a waveform to place the cursor, drag to select. Copy or cut from one track, click another, and paste. Check the tracks you want and press Merge checked to join or mix them. Export any track as WAV. The two example tracks are generated in the page so there is something to try; remove them with ✕.

Space play/stop · Ctrl+X/C/V cut/copy/paste · Del delete · Ctrl+A select all · Shift+click extend · Esc deselect · Ctrl+Z undo
